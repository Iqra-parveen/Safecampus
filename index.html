Smart Campus Reporting System

SafeCampus helps students and staff report complaints anonymously, monitor campus issues, and improve safety through a secure reporting platform. Users can securely register, verify accounts, submit complaints, and track reports through a modern smart-campus interface.

Features

Anonymous Reporting

Students and staff members can submit complaints securely without revealing their identity.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can monitor complaints, manage campus issues, and review reports efficiently.

Complaint Tracking

Users can track report progress and monitor the resolution status in real-time.
